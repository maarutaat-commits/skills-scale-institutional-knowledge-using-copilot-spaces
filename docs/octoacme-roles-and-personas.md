# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA Lead

### Role Summary
QA Leads oversee test planning, quality assurance, and release readiness. They ensure that features meet acceptance criteria and maintain high quality standards before deployment.

### Responsibilities
- Develop comprehensive test plans aligned with acceptance criteria
- Oversee test execution and manage defect triage
- Collaborate with Developers and Product Managers on quality gates
- Establish and maintain quality metrics and dashboards
- Lead acceptance testing and sign-off processes
- Plan and execute smoke tests before releases
- Document known issues and workarounds

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality releases with minimal defects
- Maintain fast feedback loops for developers
- Ensure customer satisfaction through thorough testing

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and kickoff meetings
- Daily defect triage and status updates
- Pre-release quality gates and sign-offs
- Post-incident retrospectives and action items

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Collaborate on test planning, review test coverage, investigate and resolve defects
- **Product Managers**: Clarify acceptance criteria, validate feature completeness
- **Project Managers**: Report on quality status and release readiness blockers
- **Release Manager**: Coordinate final smoke tests and release verification

---

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
UX Designers shape user experiences by designing intuitive interfaces, user flows, and interactions. They champion usability and incorporate user feedback throughout the development lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Design wireframes, mockups, and interactive prototypes
- Define user flows and interaction patterns
- Participate in design reviews and provide design feedback
- Gather and synthesize user feedback for continuous improvement
- Document design systems and design principles
- Advocate for user needs in trade-off discussions

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible, and delightful user experiences
- Reduce friction in user workflows
- Drive user adoption and satisfaction

### Typical Communication
- Design kickoffs and critique sessions
- User research findings and insights
- Design specs and component documentation
- Stakeholder demos and feedback sessions

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Align on user needs, prioritize design work, validate solutions
- **Developers**: Collaborate on implementation feasibility, review code for design adherence
- **Project Managers**: Provide design status and flag design-related risks
- **QA Lead**: Ensure usability and design acceptance criteria are tested

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate and oversee the deployment process, manage release calendars, and ensure smooth transitions to production. They own the release readiness and incident response during deployments.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain release schedules and communication plans
- Coordinate pre-release activities (build, test, sign-offs)
- Manage deployment procedures and checklists
- Monitor deployments and manage rollback procedures
- Communicate release status to stakeholders
- Document release notes and deployment artifacts
- Coordinate incident response and post-incident reviews

### Goals
- Execute reliable, predictable releases with minimal downtime
- Reduce time-to-recovery in case of deployment issues
- Maintain clear visibility and communication throughout the release cycle

### Typical Communication
- Release planning meetings and stakeholder briefings
- Pre-release readiness reviews and go/no-go decisions
- Real-time deployment coordination and status updates
- Post-release retrospectives and post-incident reviews

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Coordinate code freeze, build verification, and rollback procedures
- **QA Lead**: Verify release quality gates and smoke test execution
- **Project Managers**: Report on release status and timeline adherence
- **Product Managers**: Coordinate feature announcements and release communications
- **Customer Support Liaison**: Coordinate support readiness and customer notifications

---

## Customer Support Liaison

### Role Summary
Customer Support Liaisons represent the voice of support teams and customers within the project lifecycle. They surface customer pain points, ensure support readiness, and facilitate feedback loops for continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Channel customer feedback and support tickets into product and project discussions
- Assess support impact of new features and changes
- Ensure support teams are equipped for releases (training, documentation, FAQs)
- Identify and escalate critical customer issues
- Participate in incident response and post-incident reviews
- Provide insights on customer usage patterns and satisfaction
- Collaborate on customer-facing documentation and help content

### Goals
- Maximize customer satisfaction and reduce support burden
- Prevent support escalations through better product clarity
- Drive product improvements based on real customer needs

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and feature kickoffs
- Support readiness reviews before releases
- Customer feedback summaries and insights
- Incident response and escalation coordination
- Retrospectives and continuous improvement discussions

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Share customer insights, validate prioritization against customer needs
- **Developers**: Clarify customer use cases, flag documentation or usability gaps
- **UX Designers**: Provide feedback on user experience from support interactions
- **Release Manager**: Coordinate support readiness and customer notifications
- **Project Managers**: Escalate customer-impacting risks and blockers
